Wrecking Run Privacy Policy

Developer: Kealon · Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Wrecking Run (package com.wreckingrun.runner) collects, why, and who it is shared with. Wrecking Run is a single-player game. It has no account system of its own and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or microphone.

1. Game progress stored on your device

Your Scrap, upgrades, unlocked areas, collection and settings are saved locally on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them, unless they are also backed up with Google Play Games (see below).

2. Google Play Games (sign-in, cloud save and leaderboards)

The game signs in to Google Play Games when it is available on your device. Your game progress is then also stored as a saved game in your own Google account, so you can restore it on another device or after reinstalling. Your best distance and your Daily Wreck results are submitted to Play Games leaderboards, where other players can see your Play Games name, avatar and score. These services are provided by Google and are subject to the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Google password or email address. You can delete saved games and manage your Play Games profile and visibility in the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob: an occasional full-screen ad between runs, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a bonus. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and language, and how you interact with ads.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy options. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized ads. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In US states with privacy laws, sharing this information with ad partners can count as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under those laws. You can opt out at any time in Settings › Privacy options.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device).

4. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

An optional purchase removes the between-run ads. It is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation of what you own, so it can apply it and restore it on reinstall.

5. Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To learn how the game is played (for example how far runs go, which settings are used and how often ads are watched), the game can send usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google. Where the law requires consent (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), nothing is sent until you agree on Google's consent form, the same one used for ads, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy options. Analytics does not use your advertising ID, and events never contain your name, email address or location. Google processes this data, including your IP address and device information, under its own privacy policy (see how Google uses information from apps that use its services).

6. What we do not do

Apart from the services described above, we do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not run our own servers that collect your data.

7. Children

Wrecking Run is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to its processing. Because the game keeps your data on your device or in your own Google account, you can delete it directly as described above. For data held by Google, use the controls and contact details in Google's privacy policy. For anything else, contact us.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
